# Day 10 — Conditional Aggregation Functions 📊

**Date:** October 2, 2026

Day 10 focused on using multi-criteria math and statistical functions in Excel to summarise, count, and average data based on specific conditions.

## What I Learned & Practiced

* Applying SUMIFS to calculate totals across multiple criteria
* Using COUNTIFS for conditional counting and frequency tracking
* Implementing AVERAGEIFS to calculate filtered averages
* Combining text, numeric, and date criteria effectively across large datasets

## Practice

Created summary reports by building multi-condition aggregation models using SUMIFS, COUNTIFS, and AVERAGEIFS to filter sales and performance metrics across specific dates, regions, and categories.

## Key Takeaway

Learned how conditional aggregation functions streamline reporting by allowing precise, dynamic data summarisation without needing manual filtering.
