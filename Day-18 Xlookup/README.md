# Day 18 — Modern Lookups: Mastering XLOOKUP 📊

**Date:** October 10, 2026

Day 18 focused on mastering XLOOKUP in Excel to streamline data retrieval, take advantage of native spill arrays, and replace legacy lookup methods.

## What I Learned & Practiced

* Understanding XLOOKUP syntax, defaults, and built-in error handling (if_not_found)
* Using different match modes including exact match, wildcard searches (*, ?), and approximate matches
* Returning multi-column results across adjacent cells with a single spill formula
* Evaluating the structural advantages of XLOOKUP over VLOOKUP, HLOOKUP, and INDEX/MATCH

## Practice

Migrated legacy lookup sheets to XLOOKUP formulas, implemented wildcard queries for partial name searches, and retrieved entire multi-column customer profiles in one formula without needing manual helper indices.

## Key Takeaway

Learned how XLOOKUP eliminates the structural limitations of VLOOKUP—defaulting to exact match, searching left-to-right or right-to-left, and natively spilling multiple columns safely.
